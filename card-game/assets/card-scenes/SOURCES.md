# Moonfall: actual scene-based replacement card artwork

All **18 cards** now use separate illustrations from **Soimoi Tarot** by **Mike Koz (2025)**, licensed under **Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International**. Source: [mixvlad/TarotCards — tarot/soimoi](https://github.com/mixvlad/TarotCards/tree/main/tarot/soimoi) and the [original artist website](https://koz.tv/). License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/.

**Adaptations made:** The artwork is covered/cropped to a 420×560 fantasy card frame, with dynamic card cost, ability name and rarity frame composited at runtime, and a mild dark fade behind the readable nameplate. No original scene painting is replaced with a static character portrait or generated overlay. The underlying images are unchanged in their repository files.

The original works are tarot-style scenes and do **not** depict Moonfall-specific characters or exactly reproduce each card's story beat. The association to card abilities below is an artistic adaptation, and no endorsement from Mike Koz is implied.
